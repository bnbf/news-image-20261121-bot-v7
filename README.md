Sum up 21 November's world news in one image — a single front page for Saturday 21 November 2026, built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty `news-image-20261121`.

## What's on the page

- **3 hero leads** — Peru M6.7 quake, Kyiv hit by 400+ drones and 110 missiles, Nasdaq −5.3%
- **12 picture cards**, each with a confidence tag and its source
- **20 ALSO briefs** across 7 regions
- **6-number strip** — 6.7 | 400+ | 110 | −5.3% | 3 | 1 in 12
- **UTC timeline** of the day, from the 00:09 Peru quake to the Nasdaq close
- **World map with numbered pins** for the pictured stories
- **DATE CHECK footer** — names what is *not* 21 November news (PIF strike 19 Nov, Nvidia results 17 Nov, Voyager 1 light-day 18 Nov, Anthropic $81.3bn IPO 19 Nov, EU's €45bn Ukraine loan 19 Nov) and what had *not yet* happened (COP31 final deal, Spain votes 29 Nov)

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024×1024) to `/output`, or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set. Python 3 stdlib only — no dependencies, deterministic output.
